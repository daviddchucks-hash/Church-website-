About Our Church

Built on Faith,
Grounded in Love

Jesus Embassy is a vibrant, Spirit-filled community committed to raising disciples who impact their generation. We believe in the power of prayer, the authority of God's Word, and the transforming grace of Jesus Christ.

We are a family — diverse in background, united in purpose. Whether you are searching for meaning, healing, or a spiritual home, you have found the right place.

Major Announcement

Jesus Embassy Set to Open Brand New Worship Centre — August 2025

After years of prayer, seed-sowing, and faithful giving, God has made a way! We are thrilled to announce the completion of our new, state-of-the-art worship centre in the heart of Abuja. The 2,000-seat auditorium, equipped with modern sound systems, a dedicated children's wing, and a counselling suite, will be commissioned on the first Sunday of August 2025.
